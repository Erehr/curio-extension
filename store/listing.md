# Store listing

Texts for the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons. Not shipped in the package.

## Name

Send to Curio

## Short description

At most 132 characters.

> Save pictures, video, sound, files and links from the browser to your Curio libraries, with folders, tags and notes.

## Category

Productivity, as the store calls it; confirm the exact category names at upload.

## Description

> Send to Curio saves what you find on the web into Curio, the free desktop app for collecting and organising pictures, video, sound, documents and links. Curio must be installed and running on the same computer: the extension talks only to it, on this computer.
>
> Save
> • Right-click a picture, video, sound, link or page: Save to Curio opens the editor before saving. Use the quick-save shortcut to skip the editor.
> • Alt+click, drag onto the drop zone, or the button on pictures.
> • Screenshots of the visible page or an area, and the page itself as a link.
> • Keyboard shortcuts for each, and site rules: a library, folders and tags of their own for what you save from a site, with tags taken from the page.
>
> Choose where
> • Any library on this computer, open or closed; its folders and tags as Curio has them, with their colours.
> • The panel, in a window or the browser's side panel: folders, tags, name, notes, rating and choice fields.
>
> Gets the real file
> • The largest version a page offers (srcset, picture, known image hosts), fetched by the browser with your sign-ins, so pictures behind a login come through.
> • Files up to 2 GB.
> • A link already in the library isn't saved twice.
>
> Works when Curio is closed
> • Saves wait, with their files, until Curio runs; Open Curio starts it from the browser.
>
> Private
> • No account, no server, no analytics. Nothing is sent to the makers of the extension or to anyone else; see the privacy statement.
>
